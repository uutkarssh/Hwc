What We Offer

Programs designed for your life

Three focused wellness programs, one monthly plan — all personalised and guided by Nandini Maurya from day one.

Service 01

Weight Management

Achieving and maintaining a healthy weight is about far more than calorie counting. Our Weight Management program takes a comprehensive approach — factoring in your metabolism, lifestyle, food relationship, and daily routine to create a plan that is both effective and sustainable.

You will never be handed a generic diet chart. Nandini works with you to identify what genuinely works for your body, adjusting and refining the approach as you progress.

Personalised diet planning Weekly progress checks Sustainable results No extreme diets

Service 02

Healthy Lifestyle Coaching

Good health is built in the margins of everyday life — in your sleep patterns, your stress responses, your hydration habits, and your morning routines. This program is designed to help you take ownership of those small, consistent choices that compound into transformative change.

Through structured coaching sessions and daily guidance, Nandini helps you identify limiting habits, introduce healthier alternatives, and build a lifestyle that energises rather than exhausts you.

Habit formation guidance Sleep & stress support Energy improvement Ongoing accountability

Service 03

Wellness Consultation

Before any program begins, understanding where you are today is essential. A wellness consultation with Nandini is a focused, one-on-one conversation about your health history, current challenges, specific goals, and what has or has not worked for you in the past.

From this session, a tailored roadmap is created — one that outlines the right program, the right pace, and the right support structure for your unique situation. It is the foundation of everything that follows.

1-on-1 with Nandini Health history review Personalised roadmap Goal-setting session

Paid Monthly Plan

Everything you need, in one plan

The monthly membership gives you full, ongoing access to Nandini's guidance and a structured support system — every single day of the month.

Live Training Sessions

Participate in live, interactive sessions led by Nandini — covering movement, nutrition, mindset, and more. Scheduled regularly to fit your routine.

Daily Diet Monitoring

Share your meals and eating patterns with Nandini for daily review and feedback. Stay aligned with your dietary goals with real-time guidance.

Progress Tracking

Detailed, structured tracking of your health metrics over time — so you can see exactly how far you have come and what to focus on next.

1-on-1 Motivation Support

Direct access to Nandini for personal motivation and guidance when you need it most. No general chatbots — real, human support.

Regular Group Meetings

Connect with fellow members in structured group sessions. Share experiences, stay accountable, and draw strength from a community that understands your journey.
