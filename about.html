Our Story

Wellness from the inside out

Holistic Wellness Club was built on one belief — that lasting health comes not from quick fixes, but from understanding your body, your habits, and your mindset together.

NM

How We Began

Born from a calling to help

Holistic Wellness Club was founded by Nandini Maurya — a certified health and wellness coach who spent years working closely with individuals struggling to find balance between their health goals and the pressures of everyday life.

What started as personal consultations soon grew into a structured, community-driven program. Today, the club serves as a safe and supportive space where science-backed wellness meets human connection — guiding every member toward a healthier, more fulfilling life.

Our Mission

To make genuine wellness accessible to every individual

We believe that health is not a destination — it is a continuous, evolving journey. Our mission is to walk alongside each member with personalised guidance, consistent support, and a program that adapts to their life — not the other way around.
